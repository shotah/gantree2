Privacy policy

gantree · effective October 10, 2026

This policy covers everything that signs in with Google under the gantree name: the gantree web app (settings and chat), the agent harness and its tool plugins, and the companion phone apps (cab for Android, helm for iPhone). All of it is open-source, self-hosted software. Each copy is run by one person (the operator) on a Cloudflare account or hardware they control. The developer does not operate a server that receives, stores, or can see your Google data.

Signing in

Sign in with Google is used only to identify you. The app requests your Google account id, email address, and basic profile. It keeps your account id and email to decide whether you are on the operator's allowlist, which role you hold, and which agents you may talk to. Nothing else from your Google account is read by signing in.

What Google data an agent accesses

Only when the operator connects a Google account to an agent, and only within the scopes shown on the Google consent screen at that time. Depending on the operator's configuration this can include:

  • Gmail messages, threads, labels, and drafts; sending mail.
  • Calendar events and free/busy information.
  • Tasks and task lists.
  • Contacts (People API).
  • Drive, Docs, and Sheets files.
  • Google fitness and health data (if connected separately).
  • Basic profile (email address) to identify the connected account.

Other connected accounts

The operator may also connect non-Google accounts. The same rules in this policy apply to them:

  • Strava — activities, routes, and athlete stats, via Strava's OAuth.
  • Garmin Connect — sleep, steps, heart rate, and workouts. Garmin has no public OAuth for this data; the operator supplies their own Garmin login on their machine and the resulting session is stored with that agent. Garmin credentials are never sent to the developer or pasted into chat.

How it is used

Data is read or changed only in direct response to a request from the operator (or an allowlisted user the operator configured), or a scheduled task the operator set up. Examples: "what's on my calendar today", "find the email from the dentist", "add this to my tasks".

To answer, the relevant content (for example an email body or a list of events) is sent to the large-language-model provider the operator configured (for example Google Gemini, OpenAI, or a model running on the operator's own hardware). That provider processes it under its own terms and privacy policy, which the operator chose. gantree does not send Google data anywhere else.

Storage

  • OAuth tokens (access and refresh tokens) are stored inside that agent's container and in a private object-storage bucket the operator owns, so the agent survives a restart. They are not transmitted to the developer.
  • Google content is held in memory while a request is answered. The agent keeps a local chat history, in which case replies that quote Google content are kept with that agent until the operator deletes them.
  • The OAuth redirect page at shotah.github.io/gantree2/oauth-catch/ is static HTML shared by the Google, Google Health, and Strava connections. It displays the one-time authorization code from the URL and stores nothing; no server receives it.

Sharing and sale

Google user data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with third parties other than the operator-configured model provider described above.

Limited Use

gantree's (including the harness, its plugins, the web app, and the cab and helm phone apps) use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention

  • Google content fetched to answer a request is discarded when the request completes, except where it is quoted in the agent's chat history, which stays with that agent until the operator deletes it or retires the agent.
  • OAuth tokens are kept until revoked or until the operator deletes them or retires the agent.
  • Sign-in records (account id and email) are kept until the operator removes the user.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • Revoke at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions. The stored refresh token stops working immediately.
  • Ask the operator to disconnect the account from the agent, or to retire the agent, to remove stored credentials and history.
  • Ask the operator to remove you from the users list to erase your sign-in record.

Children

The software is not directed at children under 13 and the developer does not knowingly collect data from them. Operators decide who they allowlist.

Changes to this policy

This page is versioned with the source code in the gantree repository. Material changes update the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about this software: open an issue at github.com/shotah/gantree2/issues. Questions about a specific running instance go to the person who operates it.
